## 2. Boundary

This request can identify a bounded peer set and explain why each name belongs
in it. It cannot establish feature coverage, product quality, market rank,
commercial viability, or source-code reuse — those claims require the standard
profile, deep dossier, or comparison depths, each commissioned as its own
request or as a follow-up action on this one.
